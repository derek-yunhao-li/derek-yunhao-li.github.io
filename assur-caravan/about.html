About the game

The Assur Caravan

A classroom trading game set in 1895 BCE. Begin in Assur with borrowed silver, choose a cargo of tin and textiles, and trade in the markets of Anatolia.

Plan a route, compare prices and costs, and decide when to send your earnings home. Your aim is to finish the trading season with the highest profit secured in Assur.

Classroom use

The game is based on the Yale Assur Trade Simulation. It uses a simplified trading season to explore decisions about cargo, transport, risk, and profit. Prices and events are designed for classroom play.

Saving your game

Progress is saved in your browser. Choose Resume game on the starting page to continue a saved season. Use Save in the game to download a backup or move it to another device.
